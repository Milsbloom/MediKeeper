# MediKeeper Privacy Policy

_Effective 15 November 2026_

MediKeeper is a medication reminder app. It is built so that your health information never leaves your phone.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you enter (medications, doses, schedules, notes, the people you add, and your dose history) is stored
only on your device. We do not have servers that receive it, and we cannot see it. MediKeeper works without an
internet connection and without an account.

Reminders are scheduled by your phone's own notification system. No reminder content is sent anywhere.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. You can export your history as a CSV file, or create a PDF
report, at any time. Those files go only where you choose to share them.

## Purchases (MediKeeper Plus)

If you buy MediKeeper Plus, payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your
card details.

To confirm which purchases you have made, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a
random, anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information
(such as the app version, operating system and store country). It does not receive any of your medication or
health information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## What we don't do

- No advertising, and no ad tracking.
- No analytics or tracking of how you use the app.
- No selling or sharing of your data.
- No account, email address or phone number is needed.

## Children

MediKeeper is not directed at children under 13. A parent or carer may use it to track a child's medication;
that information stays on the parent's or carer's phone.
